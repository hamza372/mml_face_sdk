# Supported-attack statement

The SDK is designed to raise resistance to ordinary printed-photo and screen-replay presentation attempts through two MiniFAS classifiers, crop-scale diversity, temporal aggregation, and a low-score latch. This is an engineering intent, not a certification or guarantee.

No claim is made for 3D masks, deepfake injection, virtual cameras, compromised devices, adversarial examples, twins/lookalikes, extreme illumination, novel sensors, or attack media absent from validation. The current TFLite conversions and thresholds have not been independently tested to ISO/IEC 30107-3. Production release requires a documented attack matrix, corpus, APCER/BPCER reporting, device-stratified results, and external review.
